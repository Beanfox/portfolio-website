About Me

I'm Ben Brake, a Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo with hands-on experience designing multi-layer PCBs, building embedded test fixtures, and bringing hardware from schematic to soldered prototype. As an Electrical Project Manager with WARG and a former co-op at ForceN, I've led design reviews, debugged signal integrity issues with oscilloscopes, and coordinated board manufacturing for UAV flight systems.

Programming Adapter Rev. 2

WARG
  • Problem: Embedded flight systems validation required custom harnesses and power supplies for every board, causing bench clutter and debug delays.
  • Built: Designed an adapter integrating an STM32 USB-to-CAN bridge, STLINK V2/V3 programming interfaces, and a 100W USB-C PD system with DC-DC conversion.
  • Outcome: Replaced custom harnesses with a single USB-C interface to power and flash flight boards.

12S → 5.2V High Current Servo Buck Converter

WARG
  • Problem: Fixed-wing UAV required stepping down 12S battery power (36V–50.4V) to 5.2V at 8A continuous for wing aileron servos while compensating for harness voltage drops.
  • Built: Designed, simulated (LTSpice), assembled, and tested a buck board using the LM65680 controller with optimized copper pours and thermal management.
  • Outcome: Delivered stable 5.2V @ 8A output across full input voltage range for safe UAV servo actuation.

Golf Swing Sensor

Personal Project
  • Problem: Lack of accessible, clip-on training tools that provide high-rate kinematic data (swing speed, tempo, plane, face angle).
  • Built: Designed a custom 4-layer PCB in Altium with ESP32-S3 MCU, BMI270 IMU, battery management, and MicroPython firmware for BLE transmission.
  • Outcome: Compact shaft-mounted sensor transmitting real-time motion metrics to a companion mobile app.
